Where the achiever levels come from.This school’s assessment export carries no internal total, so HA, MA and LA here are worked out from the UT-01 and UT-02 unit tests of 2026–27 instead, averaged per subject, at HA above 75 and LA below 50. Unit-test marks run much higher than a full internal total — English averages 91.6% — so on the average across all four subjects 62 of the 73 students land at HA and none at LA. The levels separate meaningfully only within Mathematics (36 HA, 25 MA, 5 LA). Read them as a position on these two tests, not as a judgement on the student.
